What this version protects—and what it cannot recover

Saved reports and backups are encrypted with your passphrase. Keep that passphrase separately: there is no reset or recovery service. Clearing browser storage can remove the local copy. A downloaded backup is not verified until you reopen it successfully.

Encryption protects stored data, not an unlocked screen or a compromised device. Open recovery files only in a trusted copy of this client. The portable identity in this rehearsal is an unsigned identifier, not a verified account or signing identity.

Use Lock when you leave. An unsaved draft exists only in the form. Closing or locking this page discards it after a warning where the browser supports one. Encrypted storage can also be unavailable for local files in some browsers; use a trusted HTTPS or localhost installation in that case.

Experience is admissible; interpretation remains open.
